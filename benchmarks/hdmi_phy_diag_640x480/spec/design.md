The diagnostic retains the cube project's 50 MHz input, 25 MHz pixel clock, 125 MHz serializer clock, 640x480 active raster, TMDS control symbols, differential output pins, and B17 HDMI source power-switch enable. Its PLL uses external feedback through a BUFG, matching the supplied PH1P35 reference project's clock topology. The only architectural substitution is a combinational eight-bar color source in place of cube rendering and its double-buffered framebuffer.
